Films

Every feature Tarkovsky directed, in order. Five of them are free to watch in full on Mosfilm's official channel, with English subtitles; for the two films made abroad you can watch the official trailer.

1962

Ivan's Childhood

Иваново детство

Twelve-year-old Ivan, orphaned by the war, works as a scout behind German lines. Tarkovsky's debut cuts between the boy's sunlit dreams and the flooded, burnt landscape of the front, and won the Golden Lion in Venice.

Year
1962
Runtime
95 min
Country
USSR
Themes
Memory, war

1966

Andrei Rublev

Андрей Рублёв

The life of the fifteenth-century icon painter told in eight episodes of famine, raids and doubt. Shot in black and white, the film opens into colour only at the very end, on Rublev's icons. Censored for years, it was first cut from a longer version titled The Passion According to Andrei.

Year
1966
Runtime
183 min
Country
USSR
Themes
Faith, art, history

Full film, Mosfilm official channel. Open on YouTube

1972

Solaris

Солярис

Psychologist Kris Kelvin arrives at a space station orbiting an ocean planet that gives shape to the crew's memories, including his wife Hari, who died years before. Based on the novel by Stanisław Lem, it won the Grand Prix at Cannes.

1972
Runtime
167 min

USSR

1975

Mirror

Зеркало

A loosely autobiographical film built from childhood memories, dreams, newsreel and poems written and read by the director's father, Arseny Tarkovsky. Margarita Terekhova plays both the narrator's mother and his wife.

1979

Stalker

Сталкер

A guide known as the Stalker leads a writer and a professor through the Zone, a forbidden wasteland said to hold a room that grants a person's deepest wish. Written by Arkady and Boris Strugatsky, loosely after their novel Roadside Picnic.

1983

Nostalghia

Nostalghia

A Russian poet travelling through Italy to research an eighteenth-century composer meets Domenico, a recluse who asks him to carry a lit candle across a drained thermal pool. Tarkovsky's first film made outside the Soviet Union.

Year
1983
Runtime
125 min
Country

Themes

1986

The Sacrifice

Offret

On his birthday, a retired actor hears that nuclear war has begun and vows to give up everything he loves if the catastrophe can be undone. Tarkovsky's last film, shot in Sweden, won the Grand Prix at Cannes in the year of his death.

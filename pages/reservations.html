From first visit to booked seat

  1. Home

    The visitor learns who Tarkovsky was and what the site offers.

  2. Films

    They search, filter, read the summaries and watch a film or trailer.

  3. Reservation

    They fill in the form, check the contacts and open further reading.

  4. Confirmation

    The reservation is checked, saved in the browser and listed without reloading the page.

Scenarios

The home page loads quickly, the menu stays visible, the films page shows each film in a clear block, and external links open in a new tab so the visitor never loses their place on the site.

If an external link or a video is unavailable, the rest of the site keeps working. A missing image is replaced with a neutral placeholder, and every embedded video has an “Open on YouTube” link as a fallback.

The site uses no heavy libraries, loads images lazily, loads a YouTube player only after the visitor presses play, and explains every form error next to the form instead of reloading the page.

Risks

Team availability internal
Illness, motivation or different visions can delay the work or make the design inconsistent.
Code and assets technical
Disorganised code, heavy images or a poor colour palette can hurt speed and readability.
Hosting and requirements external
University infrastructure, unavailable servers or changing requirements can affect how the site is presented.
User acceptance audience
An overloaded design or missing features can lower the interest of the target audience.
